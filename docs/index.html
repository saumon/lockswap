Swap your locker, not your day.

In most companies a locker is assigned once and never moves. LockSwap lets colleagues find each other and agree a swap directly — then facilities validate it once the badges have been re-associated.

Everything a swap needs, nothing it doesn't.

From the first wish to the badge in the new locker: one locker, one holder — a given number on a given floor can never belong to two people.

For employees

  • Record your floor and locker number — or say you have none.
  • Say which floor you are after, and browse everyone else's wishes.
  • It's a match! — the list tags the one swap guaranteed to work both ways.
  • Propose, accept, decline or withdraw; accepting one declines every competing proposal.
  • Your locker is held still while a swap is outstanding.
  • A full proposal history, recorded as it happened.

For facilities

  • A directory of every account with floor, locker and wish — filterable four ways.
  • A validation queue: approve accepted exchanges, or refuse them with a reason.
  • The Locker Map: draw zones floor by floor, and every screen says where a locker physically is.
  • The Danger Zone: allowed e-mail domains, site language, the building's floors, the locker-number format.
  • Grant admin rights; activate an account by hand.

Under the hood

  • E-mail activation, password reset, address-change confirmation, lockout after 5 failures.
  • English and French, switched site-wide; a test fails if a key is untranslated.
  • Responsive from 320px; every screen audited with axe-core in CI.
  • Server-rendered Rails + Hotwire. No Node, no bundler, no external services.

How a swap works.

Alice holds locker 012 on floor 1 and wants floor 3. Bob holds 204 on floor 3 and wants floor 1. Five steps later, they have swapped — and the history says so.

  2. 1

    Declare a wish

    Bob

    “I'm looking for a locker on floor 1.” The list of wishes now carries his row.

  3. 2

    Spot the match

    It's a match!

    Bob is on the floor Alice wants and wants the floor she is on. The row is tagged, computed fresh every time the list is shown.

  4. 3

    Propose, accept

    Alice → Bob

    Alice proposes; Bob accepts. Both lockers are held still and every competing proposal is declined.

  5. 4

    Facilities validate

    Waiting for facilities

    The exchange waits in the validation queue. Once the badges are re-associated, an administrator validates it — or refuses it, with a reason.

  6. 5

    Swapped

    Validated

    Floors and numbers are exchanged, both wishes cleared, and the history recorded — nobody had to write it down.
